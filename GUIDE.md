# WhereToNext — how to use it

Everything for a trip in one place: the day-by-day plan, flights, hotels, a map,
what to pack, what it costs and who owes who. Plan it together beforehand, then
open it on your phone while you're there.

**New here?** Read *Signing in*, then *Your trips*. If someone sent you a link to
a trip they're planning, skip to *If you were invited* — that's the two-minute
version.

> This guide is also built into the site: **⋯ menu → 📖 Handbook**, or go to `/guide`.

---

## Signing in

The site is invite-only, so there are two ways in and one short wait the first time.

- **Continue with Google** — the usual way. Tap the button and pick your Google
  account; there's no separate password to remember.
- **Sign in with a username instead** — for anyone without a Google account. Tap
  that link under the Google button and enter the username and password you were given.

The first time you sign in you'll land on a **Request Access** screen. Leave a
short note about who you are, send it, and you'll see a "pending" screen until
you're approved. Once that happens, sign in again and you're in for good.

## Your trips

The home screen lists every trip you own or have been invited to.

- Trips are ordered by date — **the next one up comes first**.
- Finished trips drop below a **Past Adventures** divider at the bottom. Nothing
  is deleted; it just moves out of the way.
- Tap any trip card to open it.

**To start a trip:** tap **＋ New Trip**. Give it a name, a destination and dates.
You can also pick an emoji (there's a picker beside the field), choose a cover
colour or upload a cover photo, and add more destinations for a multi-city trip.
Only the name is required — everything else can come later.

> **Tip:** fill in the **Country** field if you can. It's what powers the local
> weather and the emergency information on the Overview tab.

## If you were invited

Someone else is doing the planning and you just want to follow along.

1. Open the link you were sent and sign in. The trip appears on your home screen
   automatically.
2. Start on **Overview** for the shape of the trip, then **Itinerary** for the
   day-by-day plan.
3. Check **Travel** and **Accommodations** for confirmation numbers and addresses,
   and **Packing** for anything assigned to you.
4. Got an opinion? Tap the 💬 bubble on any activity to comment or vote.

Whether you can change things depends on the role you were given — **editor** can
edit, **viewer** can read and comment. If the edit buttons aren't there, you're a
viewer.

You may also get a **read-only link** that needs no account at all. That shows the
itinerary, travel and places, and nothing private.

## Overview

The front page of a trip — a summary before you leave, and a daily briefing once
you're there.

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| ⏰ **Reminders** | Appears when something needs you: airline check-in has opened (24h before a flight), or a free-cancellation deadline is close. |
| 📍 **Today** | During the trip: today's plan, the local forecast and the local emergency numbers in one strip. **Full day →** jumps to today in the itinerary. |
| 🌤 **Weather** | Seven-day forecast for each destination. Switch °C/°F, or set a specific location with **📍 Set location**. |
| 🚨 **Emergency** | Local police/ambulance/fire numbers, the nearest hospital and police station with phone numbers, your insurance details, and links to health and visa requirements. |
| 📊 **Trip stats** | Days, events, travel legs, accommodations and budget, plus extras like air time and nights away. Hover **Destinations** to see them all. |
| 👥 **Members** | Everyone on the trip and their role. Invite people from here if you're the owner. |

> Add your own emergency contacts (the hotel desk, a local friend, your embassy)
> and your travel-insurance policy under **✎ Edit trip**. They show up on this
> card, tap-to-call on a phone.

## Itinerary

The heart of the trip: what you're doing, day by day. Two views, switched at the top:

- **🗓 By Day** — every day of the trip in order, with flights and hotel check-ins
  slotted in automatically.
- **🎭 All Activities** — everything grouped by type instead of date; handy for
  ideas that don't have a day yet.

**Adding things**

- *A quick idea:* type into the "Brainstorm this day…" box on any day and press
  Enter. It's saved as a 💭 idea you can flesh out later.
- *A real plan:* tap **＋** on a day and pick what it is — activity, food,
  sightseeing, shopping, transport, a hotel or a travel leg. Add a time, place,
  cost and notes.
- *Moving things:* drag an activity by its **⠿** handle onto another day.

**Finding your way around**

- **Destination pins** at the top of a multi-city trip (📍 Hanoi, 📍 Hội An…)
  filter to one place — you'll see only the days you're there. They're ordered by
  when you arrive. **🌍 All** resets.
- **🙈 Hide empty days** collapses days with nothing planned.
- **📅 Export to calendar** downloads the trip for Google or Apple Calendar.
- Tap 📔 on any day to write a journal entry while you're travelling.

## Travel

Flights, trains, buses, ferries and car journeys.

Tap **＋ Add** and choose the mode. Fill in where to and from, dates and times,
your airline and flight number, seat, terminal, confirmation number and cost.
Mark it **Booking confirmed** once it's paid for — confirmed and unconfirmed are
counted separately in the budget.

- Return flights pair up automatically into a single card.
- Flight length is worked out **across time zones**, so an overnight long-haul
  shows its real duration.
- Everything here appears on the matching day of the itinerary by itself.

## Accommodations

Hotels, Airbnbs, resorts — anywhere you're sleeping.

Add the property name, check-in and check-out dates, address, confirmation number
and phone. The address becomes a tappable map link, and the whole stay shows up on
the itinerary as check-in and check-out.

> **Fill in "Free cancellation until".** The card then shows a green badge while
> you can still cancel for free, turns amber in the last three days, and the
> deadline appears in your Reminders. It's the single most valuable date on a booking.

## Map

Everywhere you're going, pinned. Anything with an address or a place name gets a
pin — hotels, activities, restaurants. On a multi-city trip the destination pins
filter the map the same way they filter the itinerary.

For an exact position, paste a Google Maps link into the **Google Maps Link** field
when adding a place. That pins the precise spot instead of guessing from the name.
You can also use **📥 Import places** in the ⋯ menu to pull in a batch at once.

## Packing

A shared checklist so nothing gets left behind. Add items yourself, or start from
a template and edit it down. Tap an item to check it off; the progress counter is
on the Overview tab.

**Templates:** 🎒 Travel Essentials · 🏖 Beach / Resort · ⛷ Ski / Winter ·
🏙 City Break · 💒 Wedding / Formal Event · 🌏 International Long-Haul.

## Budget

What the trip costs, where it goes, and who owes who.

Costs you enter on flights, accommodations and activities flow in automatically.
Add anything else with **＋ Add Manual** — categories are Flights, Accommodation,
Food & Drink, Activities, Transport, Shopping and Misc, each with a planned and an
actual amount.

- **Confirmed vs Potential** — confirmed is what you've actually booked; potential
  is everything still tentative. The bar under the totals shows the split.
- **📊 Spend by Day** — a bar per day of the trip, with the priciest day
  highlighted. Hotels are spread across the nights you stay, flights count on the
  day you fly, and **Pre** collects everything booked before you left. Hover a bar
  for the detail.
- **💱 Show in** — convert the whole budget to another currency at today's rate.
  Your stored amounts don't change.
- **🤝 Splits** — tap 💸 on any expense to say who paid and who it's split between.
  The **Splits** tab then works out the fewest payments to settle everyone up.

## Travelers

Passport and document details — **private to the trip owner**.

Store passport numbers and expiry dates, Known Traveler / Global Entry numbers,
redress numbers and airline loyalty memberships. Other members of the trip cannot
see this tab, by design.

> If a passport expires within six months of your return date, a **🛂 Passport
> check** warning appears on the Overview tab — many countries refuse entry on that rule.

Use **🧳 My Travelers** in the ⋯ menu to save people you travel with often and
reuse their details on the next trip.

## Invites & sharing

Three ways to bring people in, depending on how much they need:

- **Invite by email** — open the 👥 people button and enter their email. They need
  an approved account already.
- **Invite link** — copy the link from the same panel and send it. Whoever opens
  it joins with the role you picked.
- **Public read-only link** — a link anyone can open with no account. It shows the
  itinerary, travel and places — deliberately **not** passport details, documents,
  member emails or comments. Turn it off any time and the link stops working.

**Roles:** an **editor** can add and change anything; a **viewer** can read,
comment and vote. Only the **owner** can manage members, see the Travelers tab or
delete the trip.

## Comments & votes

Decide things together without a group chat. Every activity, flight and
accommodation has a 💬 bubble. Tap it to leave a comment or vote the idea up. The
bubble shows a count when there's activity, so you can see what's being discussed
at a glance. Changes appear live for everyone with the trip open.

## While travelling

- **Today card** on the Overview: the day's plan, today's weather, tonight's hotel
  and the local emergency numbers.
- **📍 Today button** on the itinerary jumps straight to today.
- **Works offline.** Everything you've already loaded stays readable with no
  signal, and changes you make are saved and sent as soon as you're back online —
  a pill at the bottom shows anything waiting.
- **Reminders** for check-in windows and cancellation deadlines. Tap **🔔 Enable
  alerts** once to get them as notifications.
- **Journal:** tap 📔 on a day to write down how it went. Once the trip ends, the
  Overview turns into a recap with your entries.

## On your phone

It's built phone-first. Install it and it behaves like an app.

**To install:** on Android, tap **⬇ Install App** when it appears. On iPhone, open
the site in Safari and choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It then opens
full-screen with its own icon.

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| Swipe left / right | Move between tabs |
| Pull down | Refresh the trip |
| Long-press an activity | Quick actions for it |
| Drag the ⠿ handle | Move an activity to another day |
| Drag the ＋ button | Move it out of your way — it snaps to the nearest edge and stays there |

Main sections sit in the bar along the bottom; the rest are under **More**.

## Shortcuts

On a computer:

- <kbd>/</kbd> — open search
- <kbd>n</kbd> — new trip (from the home screen)
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> — close whatever is open
- Your mouse's **back button** steps back through the app, like any website

## If something breaks

**The page won't load, or spins forever.** First try a hard refresh — Ctrl/Cmd +
Shift + R. If it still hangs, the server may be asleep; tell whoever runs the site.

**I changed something and it didn't save.** The app tells you when a save fails. If
you were offline it's queued — look for the pill at the bottom of the screen and
reconnect.

**Weather or the map shows the wrong place.** Add the country to the destination
(`Plymouth, United States` rather than just `Plymouth`). For an exact spot, paste
a Google Maps link.

**I deleted something by accident.** An **Undo** button appears in the message at
the bottom for a few seconds. Deleting a whole trip asks first and can't be undone.

**I can't see the Travelers tab, or can't edit.** That's your role. Travelers is
owner-only; editing needs editor access. Ask the trip owner.
